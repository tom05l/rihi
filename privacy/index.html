Privacy Policy

Narezki Clips · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Narezki Clips is used only by its owner.

Through TikTok Login Kit it receives the owner's basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos to drafts (video.upload).

The access token is stored privately on the owner's server and is used only to upload the owner's clips. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or collected from other people.

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings.
